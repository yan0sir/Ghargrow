Homepage "Proven Machinery" photo strip.

Put your photos in this folder named 01, 02, 03 ... 10, e.g.
  01.jpg  02.jpg  03.webp  ...  10.jpg
They fill the strip in that order. 10 is the large one that grows at the end.
Any missing number shows as an empty grey box. Landscape photos work best;
they are cropped to fit each box.
